About Me

I'm an engineer based in Bangalore who likes keeping both my hands and my mind busy. I've loved electronics for as long as I can remember — there's something deeply satisfying about understanding how things work and building them myself. Outside of that, you'll usually find me out with my camera photographing birds and wildlife, or at the table tennis table. I also have a deep love for philosophy, literature, and language, and I'll happily lose an afternoon to a good book or to an idea worth turning over in my head.
